    Quick reference

    Reading the diagram

    • Columns 1–17, rows a–j. 6h means column 6, row h.
    • Holes a–e in a column are joined inside the board. So are f–j.
    • Columns 1–2: rows a–e are +, rows f–j are −.
    • Hover any part to light up everything it’s connected to. Turn on X-ray to colour every connection at once.

    LED polarity

    The flat edge on the rim is also −. A small red + marks the long leg in the diagram.

    BC547 pins

    Flat face toward you, legs down: Collector, Base, Emitter.

    Resistor for 9V

    1 LED (safe default)1kΩ
    1 LED via potentiometer470Ω
    3 red LEDs in series220Ω
    Transistor base10kΩ
    Latch cross-links47kΩ

    Know your kit

    • 1N4007 diode: the silver stripe is the − side.
    • Push buttons sit across the centre gap. If the LED stays on without pressing, turn the button 90°.
    • No capacitors in the kit, so nothing can blink or time itself.
    • Unclip the battery when you finish.
